# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

In the interest of fostering an open and welcoming environment, we as contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and considerate in communication and code reviews.
- Seek to understand before responding.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Report violations to repository maintainers.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, discriminatory language, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private or sensitive data without consent.
- Trolling, insulting comments, or sustained disruption.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the maintainers. Maintainers may take action at their discretion, including removal of content or banning users from the project.
